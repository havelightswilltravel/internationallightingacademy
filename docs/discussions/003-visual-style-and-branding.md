# Discussion 003: A more energetic look, and how much branding

## The question
The program should not feel boring. The project and service work-order workflow posters show the
style Kyle likes: dark backgrounds, bold condensed lettering, bright orange and electric-blue
accents, numbered stages, icons and real job photos. You aren't sure the logo should be
everywhere, but you'd like to see a version of both.

## What I did
The review site now has a **look switch** at the top with two versions to compare side by side:
- **HLWT look:** dark navy, glowing orange and blue accents, bold condensed headings, numbered
  stage badges like the workflow posters, and the company wordmark in the header only.
- **Neutral look:** the current clean, light, unbranded design that any licensee could use.

The logo appears once, in the header, in the HLWT look. It isn't stamped on every page.

## Options for the app itself

| Option | What it means | My view |
|---|---|---|
| A. One look for everyone | Pick one style for all companies | Simple, but a licensee doesn't want your logo, and you may not want a generic look |
| B. HLWT look only | Fully branded | Great internally; harder to sell to other companies |
| C. **Brandable look** (recommended) | Energetic design built in; each company sets its own logo and two accent colors. Your company uses the HLWT colors and logo; licensees use theirs or the neutral default. | Best of both. It also becomes a selling point: "your brand, our training." |

## Ways to make it less boring (beyond colors)
- **Process-flow lessons.** Turn key procedures (LOTO, pole-light removal, EM inspection,
  project kickoff) into numbered stage diagrams like the workflow posters, instead of long text.
- **Real photos from your jobs.** Before-and-after shots, equipment (like the sling and clevis),
  and correct vs. incorrect setups. You've already supplied some. Customer-identifying details
  need to be cropped out.
- **Short videos.** Each lesson already lists a suggested in-house video.
- **Progress and recognition.** Level-up celebrations, grade badges, streaks for finishing
  lessons, a team leaderboard for Skills Matrix ratings (opt-in), and printable certificates with
  the company brand.
- **Scenario questions.** More "you arrive on site and see…" quiz items with photos, fewer recall
  questions.

## My recommendation
Go with **C (brandable)**, with the HLWT look as your company's setting. Keep the logo to the
header, certificates and sign-in page. Spend the visual effort on process-flow diagrams, photos
and progress recognition, which make the training more engaging than color alone.

## Questions for you
1. Do you want to show both looks to Kyle and let him pick, or lead with the HLWT look?
2. Can you send the official logo files (SVG or high-resolution PNG) and brand colors? The review
   site uses a simple text wordmark as a stand-in, not the real logo.
3. Is the "Brighter places together" tagline something to use in the program, or is that
   marketing-only?
4. Are job photos cleared for training use, or do we need permission from the customer for
   photos taken on their property?
