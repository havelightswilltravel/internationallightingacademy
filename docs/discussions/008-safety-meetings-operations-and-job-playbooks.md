# Discussion 008: How the safety-meeting, operations and project material is used

## What you asked for
- **Safety meetings.** These covered both safety and operations topics. You shared them so the
  program would include:
  - our near misses and how we dealt with them
  - the regular training topics, which go deeper than general industry training
  - the operations problems that keep coming up, and how we handle them
- **Project specifics.** These should set expectations for techs on each type of job, so managers
  don't have to hand-feed the details every time.
- **Load lists.** The same idea for what a tech brings to each type of job.

## Where it went

| Material | Where it lives now |
|---|---|
| Regular safety training topics | Built into the lessons where each topic is taught (LT1-C01, C02, C06, C11; LT3-C04, C05, C06, C12; LT4-C07; and others), plus **33 toolbox talks** for safety meetings |
| Near misses and incidents | **26 case-study cards** (Library → Case studies). Each one covers what happened, why, what we changed, and two or three questions for a safety meeting. Every card names the talk or lesson it came from. |
| Operations problems | Toolbox talks such as NTE increases and first-trip completion, truck stock and parts returns, finishing paperwork and photos on site, LED driver-or-fixture write-ups, and respecting the customer site. They also appear in the case studies and the playbooks' "Common problems" sections. |
| Project specifics | LT2-C12 *Project Readiness*, the Project Specifics Sheet template, the kickoff checklist, and **10 job playbooks** |
| Load lists | **Project Load Lists: What to Bring** guide (core kit, needs by project size and job type, vehicle and trailer, recycling rules), a load list in each playbook, LT2-C12 Lesson 6 *Loading for the Job*, and hands-on skill LT2-S23 |

## The job playbooks (new)
A playbook is a one-stop expectations sheet for a type of job:
- what "done right" looks like
- what to do before leaving the shop
- the order of work on site
- common problems and how we handle them
- close-out paperwork and photos
- the load list

| Job type | Job type |
|---|---|
| Service call | Pole light removal |
| LED kit retrofit project | Emergency lighting inspection and repair |
| Fixture replacement project | Site survey |
| Exterior wall pack / area light / pole head | Sign service (LED and neon) |
| LED canopy replacement | Cooler, freezer and case lighting |

**Recommended next step:** make each playbook part of training, not just reference.
- Before a tech's first job of a given type, they read the playbook and take a short quiz on it.
- After that first job, a lead signs off a short "met expectations" checklist.

This is how the playbooks take the hand-feeding off managers.
