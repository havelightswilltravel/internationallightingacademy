# Launch checklist (publishing gate)

Nothing is published to technicians until every **BLOCKING** item below is checked. Claude will not
publish the program, or help publish it, while a blocking item is open.

## BLOCKING
- [ ] **Pay table by level established** (owner decision, Nov 20, 2026). The pay table stays outside the
      platform, but it must exist before launch.
- [ ] Program layout approved after team review, and applied to the curriculum (codes, splits, moves).
- [ ] Every new course in the layout written, with quizzes and hands-on sign-offs.
- [ ] Safety content signed off by the safety lead (SME review notes, rounds 1-6).
- [ ] Scope-by-state disclaimer and license wording reviewed (attorney and/or insurer).

## Before launch (decided Nov 20, 2026)
- [ ] Quiz integrity: block copy/paste and flag leaving the page during quizzes and exams; "this is my own
      work" pledge before each quiz and exam.
- [ ] Minimum time in program: Master cannot be awarded before 24 months; minimum time per level.
- [ ] Progression lock: techs cannot see or open lessons beyond their current level; they can always
      review earlier lessons and use search and Get Help.
- [ ] Look: HLWT look only (remove the HLWT/neutral switch); add a light/dark mode switch.
- [ ] Get Help: search our training first, then the internet; label each answer's source (our
      training, the internet, or both).
- [ ] "Show me" demonstrate modules: build a sample for the owner to review before launch.
- [ ] Video scripts (HeyGen): waiting on the owner's template; presenter list in the Video List workbook.

## Optional (owner may skip)
- [ ] Pilot: staff take 10-20 hours of courses each on a private pilot site and report problems with a
      "Report a problem" button. The owner can skip the pilot with a setting.

## Decided
- Level up as many techs as possible (no cap on LT4/LT5).
