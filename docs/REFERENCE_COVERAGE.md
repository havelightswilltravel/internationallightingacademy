# External Reference Coverage Checks

The client supplied two NALMCO training booklets. They were used **only to check that our
curriculum covers the topics an industry-certified lighting technician is expected to know.**
No text, figures, tables, examples, quiz items or chapter structure were copied or paraphrased,
and the booklets are **not stored in this repository**. Writers did not open either booklet
while drafting lessons.

| Reference | Level of the reference | How it was used |
|---|---|---|
| NALMCO apprentice-level booklet (October 2026) | Entry / apprentice technician | Topic check against LT1–LT3. Our acronym "CALT" was dropped from the LT5 title because NALMCO uses it for its own credential. |
| NALMCO *Illuminations* senior-technician textbook (November 2026) | Senior technician / crew lead | A topic-only gap analysis against LT3–LT5, with the gaps filled by original content (below). |

## Gaps found in the senior-level check and how they were filled

| Gap | Original content added |
|---|---|
| Planned maintenance as a service, for the LED era | **New course LT5-C06 "LED-Era Maintenance & Retrofit/Replacement Planning"**, covering service programs, how LED systems lose light and fail, asset inventory and failure tracking, warranty management, the retrofit-vs-replace-vs-maintain decision, cleaning, and before/after verification. It comes with 4 new hands-on skills. |
| Predicting light levels; luminaire optics and efficiency | Two new lessons in LT4-C03: the lumen method in the field, and luminaire optics, classification and efficiency. Plus a new skill to predict light levels, then verify them with a meter. |
| Upgrade strategy, life-cycle cost, controls by space type | Two new lessons in LT5-C01, plus a proposal-presentation skill. |
| Large-job logistics; hazardous materials | Two new lessons in LT5-C03, plus a waste-stream close-out skill. |
| Rolling scaffolds and specialty access | A new lesson in LT3-C06, plus a scaffold skill. |
| Smaller items: exterior egress lighting, DEHP ballasts, broken HID outer bulbs, energy-use metrics | Short additions to existing lessons. |

**Client direction applied throughout:** group relamping is an older practice that has been
replaced by LED retrofitting or fixture replacement. See `CONTENT_GOVERNANCE.md` §7.
