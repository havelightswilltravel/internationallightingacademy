To pass a year, a technician must

  1. Complete every lesson and pass every course quiz in that year at 80% or better (10 random questions per attempt; retakes draw new questions).
  2. Pass the final exam for each level (LT1–LT5) in that year at 80% or better (timed; 24-hour wait before a retake).
  3. Log the minimum field hours for each level, approved by a supervisor (see the level table below).
  4. Show every hands-on skill in that year to an evaluator, criterion by criterion. A critical safety skill fails on any safety deviation.
  5. Get the promotion approved. Hours are a minimum, not a promotion: advancement is by demonstrated skill.
