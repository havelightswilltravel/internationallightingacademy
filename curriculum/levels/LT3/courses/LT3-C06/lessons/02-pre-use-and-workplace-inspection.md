## Two Inspections Before You Go Up
ANSI A92.22 requires the operator to do two things before using a MEWP:

1. **Pre-use inspection** of the machine (including function tests) — before each shift or use
   period.
2. **Workplace inspection** — evaluating the area where the MEWP will travel and work.

Both follow the manufacturer's operating manual. If the machine has a defect, it is **removed
from service** until repaired — tagged and reported, never "worked around."

## Pre-Use Inspection — Walk-Around
Use the manufacturer's checklist (usually in the manual and on a decal). The general sequence:

**Documents and decals**
- Operator's manual in the weather-resistant storage box on the machine.
- Capacity decals, warning decals, and control labels present and legible.
- Current frequent/annual inspection decal or record.

**Chassis and power**
- Tires and wheels: damage, cuts, loose lug nuts, correct type (non-marking, foam-filled, etc.).
- Hydraulic system: leaks, damaged hoses, fluid level within the marks.
- Batteries (electric): electrolyte level if serviceable, clean terminals, secure cables,
  charger cord condition. Engine (fuel-powered): fuel, oil, coolant, leaks.
- Pothole protection (scissors): bars move freely and are not damaged.
- Outriggers/stabilizers (if equipped): condition and operation.

**Structure**
- Scissor arms, boom sections, pins and retaining fasteners: present, undamaged.
- Welds: no cracks. Structural members: no bends or dents.
- Wear pads and chains/cables (where visible): condition.

**Platform**
- Guardrails, midrails and toeboards secure; no missing pins.
- Entry gate or chain closes and latches properly.
- Platform floor clean, free of oil and debris.
- Fall protection anchorage points (booms) intact and labeled.
- Platform extension deck locks.

## Pre-Use Inspection — Function Tests
Function tests are done from the **ground controls first**, then the **platform controls**, in an
area free of obstructions and people.

| Function | What to verify |
|---|---|
| Ground controls | Raise, lower; selector switch works |
| Emergency stop (ground and platform) | Stops all functions immediately |
| Platform controls | Enable switch/foot switch required; drive, steer, raise, lower, boom functions smooth and in correct direction |
| Horn, alarms, lights | Travel/descent alarm, beacon (if equipped) |
| Tilt alarm | Sounds/locks out per manufacturer test procedure |
| Load-sensing system (if equipped) | Indicator and test per manual |
| Brakes | Machine holds on its rated slope and stops when the joystick is released |
| Drive speed limit when elevated | Machine slows when the platform is raised |
| Emergency/auxiliary lowering | Operates per manual — **you must know how to use it before you need it** |
| Pothole protection | Deploys when the platform is raised |

If any function fails, stop: lower the platform, tag the machine "DO NOT OPERATE," and report.

## Workplace Inspection
Walk the area where you'll travel and set up. Look **down, around, and up.**

**Down — the surface**
- Slope versus the machine's rated slope (decal) — many scissors are rated for little or no slope
  when elevated.
- Drop-offs, loading docks, ramps, curbs, holes, trenches, floor openings.
- Covered openings (steel plates, plywood) — can they hold the machine's weight?
- Soft ground, mud, fresh asphalt, sprinkler valve boxes, manholes, underground vaults.
- **Floor load capacity:** Elevated slabs, mezzanines, raised floors and finished gym floors may
  not support the machine's wheel loads. Check with the facility or engineer.
- Debris, cords and hoses in the travel path.

**Around — the environment**
- Pedestrian and vehicle traffic: barricades, cones, spotter.
- Other equipment: forklifts, overhead doors, cranes.
- **Weather:** wind (gusts count — compare to the machine's rated wind speed), lightning, rain,
  ice.
- Indoor air: fuel-powered MEWPs produce carbon monoxide; use electric machines indoors unless
  ventilation is adequate.

**Up — overhead**
- **Energized power lines** and electrical equipment (Lesson 4).
- Sprinkler pipes and heads, ducts, joists, beams, racking, door headers, light fixtures — **crush
  and entrapment hazards** when raising the platform or driving elevated.
- Overhead cranes and their runways.

> **Safety:** Crush and entrapment between the platform and an overhead structure kill MEWP
> operators. Raise slowly near obstructions, keep your body inside the guardrails, and watch above
> you while elevating. Many newer machines offer secondary guarding devices that stop movement if
> the operator is pushed against the controls.

## Documenting
Record the pre-use inspection on the manufacturer's or company's checklist — machine ID, date,
hour meter, items checked, defects found, and your name. Keep it with the machine or job file per
company policy. A completed checklist is evidence that the inspection actually happened.

## Key Takeaways
- Pre-use inspection every shift: documents/decals, chassis, structure, platform, then function
  tests (ground controls first).
- Know how to operate the emergency lowering system before going up.
- Any defect = remove from service, tag and report.
- Workplace inspection: look down (surface, slope, floor load), around (traffic, weather, air) and
  up (power lines, obstructions).
- Document every inspection.
