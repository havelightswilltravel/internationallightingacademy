## Spot Relamping vs. Group Relamping

| Method | What it is | When used |
|---|---|---|
| **Spot relamping** | Replace lamps one at a time as they fail | Small sites, service calls |
| **Group relamping** | Replace all lamps in an area at once, usually at about 70–80% of rated life, and clean fixtures at the same time | Large facilities; lower labor cost per lamp, uniform light levels and color |

Group relamping is planned work. Spot relamping is what most service calls involve. Either way,
the safety steps are the same.

## Before You Go Up the Ladder

1. **Identify the correct lamp.** Read the old lamp's markings and the ballast label. Confirm:
   - Lamp type and shape (T8, T5, CFL, PAR, etc.)
   - Wattage (watch for reduced-wattage /ES versions)
   - Length and base (G13, G5, Fa8, R17d, etc.)
   - CCT and CRI (match the rest of the space)
   - Ballast compatibility (the ballast label lists approved lamps)
   - For LED lamps or TLEDs: the exact type the fixture is set up for (LT2-C05)
2. **Inspect your ladder** and set it up correctly (LT1-C02).
3. **Plan the waste:** bring an empty lamp box or approved recycling container.
4. **Barricade** the area below the fixture.

## De-Energize and Verify

> **Safety:** Relamping is performed **de-energized.** Fluorescent sockets carry voltage that can
> exceed the supply voltage during lamp starting; some electronic ballasts produce several hundred
> volts at the sockets. Lamp pins can become energized when only one end is seated. Never "hot
> relamp," even if it seems faster or the customer asks.

Follow LOTO (Lesson 1): notify, switch off, lock out the breaker, try the switch, and verify absence
of voltage. For relamping, verify at the fixture's supply connection or as directed by your
supervisor. Check for emergency battery packs (often a test switch and charging indicator LED on the
fixture); a battery-fed lamp can light, and its circuit can be energized, with the breaker off.

Allow lamps and ballasts to **cool.** Fluorescent lamp ends and ballasts get hot; HID and halogen
lamps can cause serious burns.

## Relamping a Linear Fluorescent Fixture

1. **Open the fixture.** On troffers, support the door with one hand while releasing the latches;
   many doors hang on hinges or safety chains. On strip fixtures, there may be no cover.
2. **Remove the old lamp.**
   - **Medium bi-pin (G13, G5):** grip the lamp near each end and **rotate a quarter turn (90°)**
     until the pins line up with the slot, then lower it straight out.
   - **Single pin (Fa8) and recessed double contact (R17d):** push the lamp toward the
     spring-loaded socket end to compress it, then swing the other end out.
   - Never pry, twist hard, or bend the lamp. Excess force breaks lamps.
3. **Place the old lamp intact** in the recycling container right away. Do not lay it on the
   ceiling grid or floor.
4. **Inspect the sockets (tombstones).** Look for cracks, burn marks, brittleness, loose mounting,
   or sockets that do not grip the pins. Report damaged sockets; do not install new lamps in them.
5. **Inspect the fixture.** Look for melted or brittle wiring, burn marks, and a burning smell.
   Report anything unusual.
6. **Install the new lamp.** Insert the pins straight into both sockets, then rotate a quarter turn
   until it locks. You should feel it seat. Spring-loaded sockets: compress, insert, and release.
7. **Clean the lens or louver** if part of the job, using the cleaner the customer approves.
8. **Close the fixture** carefully, making sure no wires are pinched.

## Restore and Test

1. Remove your lock following the restoration steps in Lesson 1.
2. Turn the fixtures on and check that every lamp lights fully. A lamp that does not light, or only
   glows at the ends, may not be seated, may be the wrong type, or may indicate a ballast problem.
3. If new lamps do not solve the problem, **do not keep swapping parts.** Report it on the work order
   for troubleshooting (LT2-C04 and LT2-C06).

## Relamping Other Lamp Types

| Lamp | Key points |
|---|---|
| Screw-in (A19, PAR, LED) | Turn clockwise until snug; do not overtighten. Check the fixture's maximum wattage label. |
| Pin-based CFL | Match the exact base (G24d vs. G24q); pull straight out and push straight in. |
| Halogen | Let cool; wear gloves; never touch quartz; replace any missing protective lens. |
| HID | Let cool; follow MH open/enclosed rating rules; replace the lens if the fixture requires one. |

## After the Job

- Count lamps removed and installed and record them on the work order.
- Label and close the recycling container (Lesson 5).
- Leave the area clean: no lamp sleeves, packaging, or dust on desks and floors.

## Key Takeaways
- Match lamp type, wattage, base, CCT, and ballast compatibility before relamping.
- Always relamp de-energized: LOTO, try, and verify. Watch for emergency battery packs.
- Bi-pin lamps rotate a quarter turn; spring-loaded sockets compress. Never force a lamp.
- Inspect sockets and wiring, and report damage instead of installing lamps in bad sockets.
- If new lamps do not fix the problem, stop and report it for troubleshooting.
