> policy. The harness and lanyard manufacturer's inspection instructions always apply; where
> they are stricter, they govern.

*Company checklist: adapted from the company safety handbook (Safety Railings and Other Fall
Protection) and the company fall protection training.*

## When to use

- **Before every use** of a harness, lanyard, or self-retracting lifeline (SRL).
- Before going up in a boom lift or bucket truck (harness worn at all times) and any time
  you will be exposed to a fall at an unprotected edge.
- In addition to the periodic inspection by a competent person (at least yearly under ANSI
  Z359, or more often if the manufacturer requires).

## Safety first

- You must inspect your personal fall protection before each use and remove damaged or
  defective equipment from service (29 CFR 1910.140(c)(18); 1926.502(d)(21)).
- **Company rules:** every harness has **relief steps (trauma straps) installed**; every
  arrest lanyard has a **deceleration device**; **100% tie-off** with a double-leg lanyard
  when moving between anchors; anchors rated **5,000 lb** or engineered and tagged; tie off
  **high and tight**, at shoulder height whenever possible; with a 6-ft lanyard you need
  **18½ ft** below the anchor.
- Any equipment that has **arrested a fall** is removed from service, even if it looks fine.
- Never modify a harness or lanyard.

## Tools needed

- Your company-issued harness, lanyard(s), and/or SRL with manufacturer's instructions
- "DANGER - DO NOT USE" tags
- Whistle and phone for the job (part of your rescue plan)

## Checklist

### Harness

| # | Item | Fail when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Labels** present and legible (model, date, standard) | Missing or unreadable label |
| 2 | **Webbing**, inch by inch, bent in a U to open the fibers | Cuts, frays, burns, melted spots, chemical or paint damage, hard or stiff areas, discoloration, excessive wear |
| 3 | **Stitching** | Pulled, broken, cut, or missing stitches |
| 4 | **D-rings** (back, and any front or side) | Cracks, bends, sharp edges, corrosion; back D-ring does not stand up freely |
| 5 | **Buckles and adjusters** | Bent, cracked, corroded, or will not hold adjustment |
| 6 | **Grommets and keepers** | Missing, loose, or torn-out grommets |
| 7 | **Impact indicator** (if built in) | Deployed or torn |
| 8 | **Relief steps / trauma straps** | Missing, damaged, or not installed per the manufacturer |

### Lanyard or SRL

| # | Item | Fail when |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Shock absorber / deceleration pack** | Pack opened, torn, stretched, or impact indicator deployed |
| 10 | **Lanyard webbing or rope** | Cuts, frays, burns, knots, chemical damage |
| 11 | **Snap hooks and carabiners** | Gate does not self-close **and** self-lock; cracks, bends, corrosion |
| 12 | **Double-leg lanyard** | Either leg fails any item above |
| 13 | **SRL** | Housing cracked; line does not retract smoothly; does not lock on a sharp pull; indicator showing; service date overdue |

### Fit and tie-off (before you go up)

| # | Item | Correct when |
|---|---|---|
| 14 | **Fit** | Back D-ring between the shoulder blades; chest strap at mid-chest; leg straps snug (a flat hand, about three fingers, fits under); no twisted webbing; loose ends in keepers |
| 15 | **Connection** | Lanyard to the **back D-ring**; connector gates closed and locked |
| 16 | **Anchor** | Rated 5,000 lb or engineered and tagged; never conduit, pipe, fixtures, ductwork, or guardrails not rated as anchors; on a lift, the platform's designated anchor |
| 17 | **Clearance** | At least 18½ ft below the anchor with a 6-ft lanyard (check the manufacturer's chart); otherwise another method |
| 18 | **Rescue** | Rescue plan known; someone knows you are up; whistle and phone on you |

## If an item fails

1. **Do not use it.** Do not "make do" with a damaged leg or a borrowed harness that does
   not fit.
2. **Tag it "DANGER - DO NOT USE"** and remove it from service.
3. **Report it to your supervisor immediately** (company policy) and get a replacement
   before the work at height starts.
4. Equipment that arrested a fall is turned in, not reused.

## When to escalate

- No rated anchor, or not enough clearance: stop and call your supervisor for another plan.
- A customer's anchor with no tag or rating: treat it as unrated.
- Any fall or near miss: report it right away.

## Documentation

- Note harness and lanyard inspection on the job's pre-task or safety sheet if your
  supervisor requires it.
- Record any equipment removed from service: item, defect, and who it was reported to.
- Competent-person periodic inspections are recorded per the manufacturer and company program.
