*Company checklist — mirrors the company Lighting Survey Worksheet (exterior survey form). Use it
as the checklist for an exterior inspection and as the data sheet for a retrofit or fixture
replacement quote. Lesson: LT5-C06, "Surveying a Site with the Company Checklists."*

## When to use it

- Exterior inspection or night survey (lights out, damage, aiming, controls)
- Retrofit or fixture-replacement quote for parking lot, building-mounted, canopy and sign lighting
- Before/after documentation for a service program

## Safety first

- Survey from the ground first. Wear a high-visibility vest in parking lots and drive lanes;
  use cones when you stop a truck or lift in traffic.
- Do not touch poles, hand-hole covers or fixtures that look damaged, leaning, rusted through at
  the base or have exposed wiring — keep people away and report it the same day.
- Opening a hand hole, fixture or junction box to read labels is electrical work: **LOTO and
  verify absence of voltage (live-dead-live)** first, or record "not verified" and leave it for
  the repair visit. Energized checks are for qualified persons only, with NFPA 70E PPE.
- Lift or bucket truck use only if you are trained and authorized, with fall protection per the
  company policy (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.67 / 1926.453, ANSI A92).
- Night surveys: work in pairs where possible, and stay aware of traffic and site security.

## Tools needed

Survey worksheet (paper or tablet), site map or aerial printout, camera/phone, tape measure or
measuring wheel, laser distance/height meter, caliper or tape for pole sizes, binoculars,
flashlight, light meter (if light levels are part of the job), high-visibility vest.

## Header

- [ ] **Job Name** — site name (internal; never in shared training material)
- [ ] **Job Address** and **City, State**
- [ ] **Date** (and day or night survey)
- [ ] **Location #** — the customer's site or location number
- [ ] **Surveyed By**

## Columns (one row per fixture group: same type, location and mounting)

| Field | What to look for and record |
|---|---|
| Fixture Location | Area on the site map: front lot, rear drive, building east wall, canopy, sign, entry. Number the poles on the map. |
| Fixture Type | Shoebox/area light, flood, wall pack, canopy, bollard, sign, decorative post top, etc. |
| Lamp Type/Watts | Existing source and wattage (MH 400 W, HPS 250 W, LED 150 W) from the label or lamp — sizes the LED replacement |
| Fixture Height | Mounting height (pole height plus base); decides ladder, lift or bucket truck |
| Fixt Qty | Number of fixtures in this row's group |
| Pole QTY | Number of poles; note heads per pole (single, 2 at 180°, 4 at 90°) in Comments |
| Fixture Mount Type/Size | Arm (straight, mast arm, length), slip-fitter/tenon (size), yoke/trunnion, wall bracket, pendant |
| Square or Round Pole | Shape — decides which arm or adapter fits |
| Tapered Pole Size at Base and Top | Measure both ends; straight poles have the same size twice. Needed for tenon adapters, arms and replacement poles |
| Can Size | Recessed can diameter in canopies or soffits (for retrofit kits or trims) |
| Voltage | 120, 208, 240, 277 or 480 from the label or panel schedule; mark "not verified" if you could not confirm |
| Photo# | Photo of fixture, mounting, label and any damage |
| Comments | Lights out, day-burning, cycling, broken lens, poor aim, rust, pole damage, base condition, controls (photocell, time clock) |

## Exterior inspection items to add in Comments

- [ ] **Lights out** — count per group (the form's "# out when surveyed")
- [ ] Day-burners (photocell or time clock problem)
- [ ] Cycling (HID end of life or LED thermal/driver issue)
- [ ] Broken, yellowed or missing lenses and doors; water inside fixtures
- [ ] Heads mis-aimed or twisted on the arm
- [ ] Pole condition: leaning, impact damage, rust at the base, missing hand-hole covers, loose or missing anchor-bolt nuts or covers
- [ ] Wall packs: gaskets, mounting, conduit and box covers
- [ ] Controls: photocell location and condition, time clock settings, contactor location
- [ ] Light-level concerns (dark spots at entries and parking) — measure if in scope

## General Comments and Totals

- **General Comments:** access issues, lift requirements, traffic or hours restrictions, landscaping
  or trees in the way, where panels and controls are.
- **Totals:** the form tallies existing lamp types (by lamp code), lamp guards, ballast type and
  **# out when surveyed**. In the retrofit era, use the tallies to size LED replacements and the
  outage count to show the customer the current condition. Lamp codes on older versions of the
  form are fluorescent and metal halide; record whatever source is actually installed.

## When to escalate

- Any damaged, leaning or unstable pole, exposed wiring or missing hand-hole cover (make safe and report immediately)
- Many lights out on one circuit (circuit or controls problem, not lamps) — see the Library guide *Pole Light Out - Whole Circuit vs. Single Pole*
- Voltage cannot be confirmed from labels or the panel schedule

## Documentation

Turn in the worksheet, the marked site map and numbered photos. Every row needs location, type,
existing source/watts, height, quantity, mounting and voltage (or "not verified"). Keep customer
names and addresses on the work order only.
