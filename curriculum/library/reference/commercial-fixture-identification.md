> names vary a little by region and supply house; when in doubt, describe it and attach a photo.

*Company reference: adapted from the company commercial fixture chart and "Lighting 101" product
training. Taught in LT1-C05 Lesson 7.*

## How to use this guide

- Write the **common name first** on the work order, then size, count, mounting height,
  location and a photo: "Wallpack, about 14 ft, east wall at dock door 2, LED."
- The name tells the office the **access** (ladder, lift, bucket truck), the **likely parts**, and
  what a **matching replacement** should look like.
- "Typical sources" lists what you may find (legacy fluorescent, HID or incandescent) and what is
  installed today (LED). Note which one you actually find.

## Indoor fixtures

| Name | Also called | Where found | Typical sources | Service notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strip light | Strip, channel, industrial strip | Stockrooms, back rooms, mechanical rooms, coves | T8/T12 fluorescent; TLED or integrated LED | Ladder. Note length (4/8 ft), lamp count, surface or suspended |
| Wraparound | Wrap, wrap fixture | Corridors, stairwells, back offices | T8/T12 fluorescent; TLED or integrated LED | Ladder. Wrap lenses are often discontinued; note lens condition and count matching fixtures in the area |
| Troffer | Lay-in, 2x4, 2x2, 1x4, grid fixture | Offices, schools, healthcare | T8/T12/T5 fluorescent; TLED, LED retrofit kit or integrated LED | Ladder. Note size, door type (lens, parabolic louver, basket), lamp count |
| Linear high bay | T5 high bay, fluorescent high bay | Warehouses, gyms, big-box stores | T5HO or T8 fluorescent; integrated LED | Lift. Note mounting height, lamp count, cord-and-plug or hardwired |
| HID high bay | Metal halide high bay, dome high bay | Older warehouses and industrial plants | Metal halide (some HPS or mercury vapor) | Lift; let it cool. Note lamp wattage, shape and ANSI code; usually written up for LED |
| LED high bay | "UFO" high bay, round high bay | Warehouses, big-box retail, gyms | Integrated LED | Lift. Note wattage, catalog number, driver label, sensor if present |
| Recessed can | Downlight, can light, pot light | Lobbies, offices, retail, restaurants | Incandescent/halogen BR, PAR, A-lamp; pin-based CFL; LED lamp, retrofit module or integrated LED | Ladder. Note aperture size, lamp shape and base, IC or non-IC, trim type |
| Pendant | Hanging fixture, dome pendant | Restaurants, lobbies, open offices, shops | Incandescent, CFL, metal halide (industrial); LED | Ladder or lift. Note hanging height, shade type, cord or stem |
| Surface mount | Drum, flush mount, ceiling mount | Hallways, stairwells, restrooms, utility areas | Incandescent, CFL, circline; integrated LED | Ladder. Note size, shape, lens; check for emergency battery |
| Track lighting | Track heads, track | Retail sales floors, displays, galleries | Halogen PAR/MR16, metal halide heads; LED lamps or heads | Ladder. Track adapters are system-specific; note track type and head count |
| Linear slot | Slot light, architectural linear | Modern offices, retail, lobbies | T5 fluorescent; integrated LED | Ladder. Note run length, section count, driver location |
| Wall sconce | Sconce | Corridors, lobbies, stairwells, restrooms | Incandescent, CFL; LED | Usually no ladder. Note style, glass, mounting height; some are emergency units |

## Exterior fixtures

| Name | Also called | Where found | Typical sources | Service notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wallpack | Wall pack, perimeter light | Building walls, dock and back doors | HPS, metal halide, CFL; integrated LED | Ladder or lift. Note height, photocell, emergency (battery) option |
| Flood light | Flood, floodlight | Facades, signs, large areas, sports | Metal halide, halogen T3; integrated LED | Often lift. Note mounting (yoke, knuckle, slipfitter) and aiming before removal |
| Area light | Shoebox, parking lot light, pole light | Parking lots, large outdoor areas | Metal halide, HPS; integrated LED | Bucket truck. Note pole number, heads per pole, arm type, pole height and base condition |
| Canopy light | Canopy, gas station light | Gas stations, entrances, loading areas, drive-throughs | Metal halide; integrated LED | Lift and traffic control. Note deck height and cutout size and shape |
| Bollard | Bollard light, post light | Walkways, entrances, landscaping | CFL, HID, incandescent; LED | No ladder. Note diameter, height, head style, vehicle or mower damage |
| Building mount decorative | Architectural wall light, cylinder, lantern | Entrances, walkways, storefronts | CFL, metal halide, incandescent; LED | Ladder. Note style and finish for a matching order |
| Recessed can, exterior | Soffit light, overhang can | Overhangs, soffits, covered entries | Incandescent, CFL; LED | Ladder. Must be damp or wet rated; note aperture and corrosion |
| Step light | Stair light, wall recessed step light | Stairs, ramps, pathways | Incandescent, CFL; integrated LED | No ladder. Note size, faceplate style, how many on the run |
| In-ground / uplight | Well light, in-grade, uplight | Facades, trees, flagpoles | Halogen PAR, metal halide; integrated LED | No ladder. Note diameter, aiming, water inside, damaged lens |
| Street light | Cobra head, roadway light | Roads, large lots, campuses | HPS, mercury vapor, metal halide; integrated LED | Bucket truck and traffic control. Confirm the owner; some are utility-owned |
| Pole top decorative | Post top, acorn, lantern | Campuses, malls, pedestrian areas | Metal halide, HPS, CFL; LED | Ladder or lift. Note style, globe condition, pole number |
| Sign light | Sign flood, gooseneck | Building and monument signs, identifiers | Halogen PAR, metal halide, CFL; LED | Ladder or lift. Note aiming and the sign's time clock or photocell |

## Telling similar fixtures apart

- **Strip vs. wraparound vs. linear high bay:** narrow with bare lamps (strip); lens wraps
  bottom and sides (wraparound); wide, 4–6 lamps with reflectors, hung high (linear high bay).
- **Wallpack vs. flood vs. area light:** fixed flat to the wall (wallpack); aimable head on a
  knuckle or yoke (flood); on a pole arm over a lot (area light).
- **Recessed can vs. exterior can:** the exterior can is in a soffit or overhang and carries a
  damp or wet rating.
- **Bollard vs. step light:** freestanding post (bollard); set into a wall or riser (step light).

## Safety reminders

- Plan access from the fixture type: high bays, area lights, street lights and many floods need
  a lift or bucket truck and a trained operator; canopy and lot work needs traffic control.
- Lock out and verify absence of voltage before opening any fixture. Let HID fixtures cool.
- Do not service fixtures that belong to the utility (some street lights); report them.

## Documentation

Record on the work order: fixture name, size or wattage, quantity out and quantity total in the
area, mounting height, location (room, wall, pole number), source found (fluorescent, HID,
LED), and photos of the fixture and its label.
