## What this job is

A multi-day project converting existing interior fixtures (troffers, strips, wraps) to LED using
**listed retrofit kits** installed in the existing housings. The crew works from a survey and a
project specifics sheet, one crew leader owns paperwork, communication and quality, and the job was
bid at a production rate that covers all paid time.

## What "done right" looks like

- Every kit is installed per the kit's instructions and listing, with splices inside the fixture
  wiring compartment and slack trimmed and dressed.
- Every fixture matches the approved **sample install**: mounting, wire dressing, labels, lens and trim
  fit, no fingerprints.
- The survey was checked against the site on day one and differences were reported immediately.
- A **daily recap** went in every shift: done, remaining, counts, problems, near misses.
- Skipped fixtures are marked and listed with location and reason.
- Lamps and ballasts are recycled the company way; suspect PCB ballasts are kept separate and counted.
- The space looks better than when we arrived, and the manager signed off on a final walk.

## Before you leave the shop

- Read the project specifics sheet, survey and service order **like an owner**. List your questions and
  ask them before kickoff.
- Compare the survey count with the material list; report differences.
- Know the site contact, after-hours supervisor contact, work hours, blackout times, access, keys and
  lock-up procedure.
- Confirm lifts and ladders for each area, that they fit through doors and elevators, and where they are
  stored and charged.
- Load drills and bits, meters and lockout kits **for every person**, plus supplies (see the load list).
- Open any crushed or torn carton at the shop, photograph damage and report it.
- Secure the load; check hitch, chains and lights if towing.

## On site

1. Check in with the site manager; agree on the game plan, storage, recycling location and areas that
   must look normal by opening. Take **before photos**.
2. Count material against the order on arrival; report missing or damaged material immediately.
3. Crew leader does the **sample install** and gets it approved.
4. Every shift: review the JHA, inspect lifts, barricade below lifts and ladders, cover desks and food.
5. **Lock out and verify live-dead-live on every circuit** before working on conductors. Get site
   approval before shutting lighting off.
6. Grid ceilings: lift tiles with clean hands, do the dirty work, wipe the new fixture, wash up, then
   replace tiles.
7. Crew leader QC checks everyone's work every shift. Step back and look from the floor.
8. Out-of-scope requests are written down and passed to the supervisor, not just done.
9. End of shift: clean up, restore circuits, test lights, tell the site contact what is next.

## Common problems we see and how we handle them

From our company's past project files (LT2-C12 Lesson 5) and safety meetings:

- **The survey said two-lamp fixtures; many had four.** Match what is in each fixture, record where the
  differences are, and report the shortfall as soon as possible (case study *Survey Said Two-Lamp
  Fixtures*).
- **Fixtures over partitions.** Hard fixtures are usually already in the bid. Ask before skipping; mark,
  leave and list only those that truly cannot be reached without damage (case study *Fixtures Over
  Partitions*).
- **Emergency ballasts nobody planned for.** Never cut out or add emergency equipment without
  direction; label and leave, rewire or replace from stock as directed, and report the count and type
  (case study *Emergency Fixtures Nobody Planned For*).
- **Kits that do not fit the housing.** Use the approved securing method on the sheet; if you are not
  sure it will stay secure, do not install it and call.
- **The manager asked for a spotter.** Do not argue. Work elsewhere, tell your supervisor, and resume
  lift work once it is settled (case study *The Manager Who Wanted a Spotter*).
- **Fingerprints on tiles and ceilings.** Follow the clean-hands tile sequence (toolbox talk *Leave It
  Cleaner - Fingerprints, Bugs and Professionalism*).

## Paperwork and photos at close-out

- Final counts on the service order: kits, drivers, disconnects, sockets, hardware, truck stock used.
- Survey corrected; skipped fixtures listed with reasons.
- Leftover good material counted, packed, labeled and shown to the manager.
- Recycle pallets stacked, shrink-wrapped, labeled ("1 of 3") and **photographed**; PCB ballast count.
- Rental lifts called off; call-off number recorded.
- **After photos** matching the before photos; service order signed (and stamped if required).
- Paperwork photos: page alone, flat, in focus. Checklist and paperwork hand delivered to the supervisor
  with your feedback for the next sheet.

## Load list

**Core kit** (every project): 6 ft and 8 ft ladders, wire nuts (BWN) and lever connectors, spray bottles, cleaner concentrate and the 5-gallon cleaner container, clean rags, drop cloths, vacuum (or the site's), red cart, trash cans with wheels, large garbage bags, tape gun and tape, recycle boxes (project lamps / 50 = boxes), black marker, shrink wrap, cones and caution tape, and a tablet with recaps and timecards loaded. Your own drill, bits, meter and lockout kit for every person.

**LED kit retrofit add-ons** (from the company's LED conversion and retrofit-kit lists; scale to the survey):

- Kits, drivers and sockets as per the supervisor's instruction and the service order
- Wire nuts by quantity per fixture or ballast at each location; lever connectors or in-line disconnects where the sheet calls for them
- Tek screws and butterfly clips (confirm use); #18 solid wire for jumpers per the kit instructions
- Strip kits for 8 ft fixtures if in scope; 8 ft recycle boxes if 8 ft lamps come out
- Plenty of tape and 2-3 tape guns per crew, shrink wrap for the recycle pallet, 3-5 extension cords
- Ladders and lifts for each area; rope locks and a spare plug for scissor-lift repairs if lifts are used
- Twist-lock or freezer sockets and fluorescent ballasts or sockets, only if the project specifics call for it
