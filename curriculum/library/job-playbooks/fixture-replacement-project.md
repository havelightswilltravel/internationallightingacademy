## What this job is

A project that removes existing interior fixtures and installs **new complete fixtures**: troffers,
surface wraps, strips, high bays, downlights. Compared with a kit retrofit, you handle more weight,
more demolition and more mounting decisions, and every new fixture must be supported correctly.

## What "done right" looks like

- Every fixture is **supported from the box or the structure** as the manufacturer and code require.
  Conductors and wire nuts are never supports.
- Fixtures are centered and in straight rows. If the old ones were crooked or a fixture cannot be
  centered, you **asked before installing**.
- Wiring is concealed: every splice in a box, the fixture wiring compartment or a raceway.
- The finished space matches the sample install, with no fingerprints and nothing left on desks or floors.
- Counts, survey corrections, recycling and photos are complete at close-out.

## Before you leave the shop

- Read the project specifics sheet, survey and service order; list and ask your questions.
- Know the ceiling types by area (grid, drywall, open structure) and the approved mounting method for
  each. Bring a stud finder and the right toggles and hardware.
- Confirm lifts and ladders for each area and that they fit doors and elevators. Plan floor protection
  if a scissor lift will run on tile.
- Inspect new fixture cartons at the shop; photograph and report damage.
- Confirm how removed fixtures are handled: recycling, dumpster rules, pallets and pickups.
- Load tools for every person and secure the load.

## On site

1. Check in, walk the job with the manager, agree on the game plan and staging, and take **before
   photos**, including any existing damage to tiles, doors and floors.
2. Count material on arrival; report shortages and damage right away.
3. Crew leader does the sample install and gets it approved.
4. Barricade below lifts and ladders. Use a spotter for interior lift work during business hours and
   when bringing a lift through a door.
5. **Lock out and verify live-dead-live** before disconnecting the old fixture.
6. Use team lifts for heavy or long fixtures. Remove the old fixture without damaging the ceiling.
7. Drywall: use toggles or fasten to framing; **hand-tighten the last turn** until flush, then stop.
   Crumbling drywall will not hold even a toggle; go to the framing.
8. Wire, dress and conceal, restore power and test. Wipe the new fixture and replace tiles with clean
   hands.
9. QC every shift; daily recap to the supervisor.

## Common problems we see and how we handle them

- **Fixtures held up by wire nuts, or screwed straight into drywall.** On our jobs, one new fixture was
  mounted with self-tapping screws into drywall, and another had pulled loose and was held up only by
  tight wire nuts. Use toggles or framing and hand-tighten (case study *Fixtures Held Up by Wire Nuts
  and Drywall Screws*).
- **Cracked tile and door damage.** Our crews have left cracked floor tile behind a scissor lift, and we
  risk blame for door damage we did not cause. Use plywood or a lighter lift, and photograph doors
  first (case study *Cracked Tile and Disputed Door Damage*).
- **Survey does not match the site.** Report differences on day one (case study *Survey Said Two-Lamp
  Fixtures*).
- **Night work where someone else controls access.** Give security the schedule ahead of time and tell
  them about changes (case study *Night Work in a Parking Structure*).
- **Emergency fixtures not in the bid.** Do not remove or add emergency equipment without direction
  (case study *Emergency Fixtures Nobody Planned For*).

## Paperwork and photos at close-out

- Final counts on the service order; survey corrected; skipped fixtures listed with reasons.
- Removed fixtures and recycle material handled per the sheet; pallets labeled and photographed.
- Leftover good material counted, labeled and shown to the manager.
- Rental lifts called off with the call-off number recorded.
- After photos matching the before photos; final walk with the manager; service order signed.
- Paperwork, checklist and feedback hand delivered to the supervisor.

## Load list

**Core kit** (every project): 6 ft and 8 ft ladders, wire nuts (BWN) and lever connectors, spray bottles, cleaner concentrate and the 5-gallon cleaner container, clean rags, drop cloths, vacuum (or the site's), red cart, trash cans with wheels, large garbage bags, tape gun and tape, recycle boxes (project lamps / 50 = boxes), black marker, shrink wrap, cones and caution tape, and a tablet with recaps and timecards loaded. Your own drill, bits, meter and lockout kit for every person.

**Fixture replacement add-ons** (from the company's fixture-install and remove-and-replace lists; scale to the survey):

- New fixtures counted against the survey; report damaged cartons before leaving the shop
- In-line fixture disconnects, one per fixture plus spares; wire nuts or lever connectors
- Mounting hardware for each ceiling type: toggle bolts with washers and anchors, as the sheet allows
- #18 solid wire (yellow preferred) where the sheet calls for it
- Seismic support material (earthquake ties, lag bolts and the matching bits) where the site requires it (confirm)
- Boxes and garbage bags for removed fixtures; stud finder; floor protection under lifts
- Spare sockets, only if the project specifics call for it
