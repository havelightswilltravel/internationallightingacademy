## What happened

One of our technicians noticed a damaged pole base during a replacement and reported it to the site manager and the office before starting the work. This is a good catch, not an incident.

## Why it happened

He looked at the base, anchor bolts and pole before working, as our exterior rules require.

## What we changed

- Inspect and photograph the base, anchor bolts, hand hole and pole before replacing anything.
- Report damage to the site manager and the office first, then work.
- The report protected the company from being blamed and protected the next person from a pole that could fall.

## What you would do

1. What do you look at on a pole before you set up the truck?
2. Who do you tell, and how do you document it?
3. What would happen if the damage were found after we left?
