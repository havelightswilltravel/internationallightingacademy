## What happened

One of our lift trucks was totaled when its front-mounted bucket caught a structure at a restaurant site while the driver was turning. Nobody was hurt.

## Why it happened

Our lift trucks carry the bucket and a step platform hanging off the front. They swing wide and grab signs, canopies, poles and parked cars when you turn the wheel and when you back up. Drivers who do not drive these trucks often are most at risk.

## What we changed

- Watch the front-end swing on every turn and when backing.
- Use an observer to back whenever possible.
- Stow the jib before you drive.
- The event is part of why our driving lessons say driving is the riskiest part of a tech's day: in less than a year our company had two vehicles totaled.

## What you would do

1. Where on a tight lot would the bucket swing into something before you saw it?
2. When was the last time you asked for an observer to back?
3. What would make you slow down when you feel rushed to the next job?
