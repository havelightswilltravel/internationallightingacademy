## What happened

A photo from one of our jobs showed a bucket truck set up at a storefront with the boom over the customer entrance and cones only at the rear bumper. Customers were walking in and out beneath the basket. Nobody was hurt.

## Why it happened

The work zone was set up for the truck, not for what was under the bucket. There had been no plan with the site manager for the entrance.

## What we changed

- Cone off the whole area around the truck and under the bucket, not just behind it.
- Talk to the site manager first: close or redirect the entrance, use another door, or work after hours.
- If people keep walking into the zone, stop work.

## What you would do

1. Looking at the photo, what would you have done before raising the boom?
2. What would you say to a manager who does not want the entrance closed?
3. At what point do you stop work?
