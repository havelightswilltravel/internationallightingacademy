## What happened

A fixture replacement in a parking structure could only happen at night, in areas security had coned off.

## Why it happened

Someone else controlled the crew's access, so any change in the crew's schedule affected security's plan.

## What we changed

- The crew gave security the schedule ahead of time and told them about any changes.
- Rule: when someone else controls your access, your schedule is their schedule, so keep them informed.

## What you would do

1. Who controls access on your current job?
2. What happens if you show up on a night they did not expect you?
3. What else changes about safety when you work at night?
