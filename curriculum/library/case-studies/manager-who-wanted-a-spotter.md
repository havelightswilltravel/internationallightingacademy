## What happened

During a project, a site manager asked the crew to use a spotter for lifts.

## Why it happened

The site had a safety expectation the crew had not planned for.

## What we changed

- When a site asks for a safety measure, do not argue.
- Do other work that does not need the lift, get the manager's name and request to your supervisor right away, and work the lift only once it is settled.
- If a site rule is stricter than ours, follow it or stop and call your supervisor.

## What you would do

1. How would you rearrange the crew while it gets settled?
2. What do you tell your supervisor?
3. What other site rules might be stricter than ours?
