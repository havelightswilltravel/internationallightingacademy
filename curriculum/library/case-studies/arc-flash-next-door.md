## What happened

On one of our job sites, a utility lineworker was hospitalized after an arc-flash explosion in the utility's equipment while our crew was working nearby. Our crew was not involved, but they saw how fast it happened.

## Why it happened

Our materials do not describe the utility's root cause. The lesson our company drew is about the energy involved: it is far beyond anything daily-wear clothing can handle.

## What we changed

- Stay inside your qualified scope; read the arc-flash label before opening any cover.
- Arc-rated clothing must be rated at or above the incident energy; ratings are not added up.
- Keep your crew and the public away when the utility is working.

## What you would do

1. What is our company limit on which panels you may work in?
2. What would you do if the utility starts work next to your job?
3. What do you look for on an arc-flash label?
