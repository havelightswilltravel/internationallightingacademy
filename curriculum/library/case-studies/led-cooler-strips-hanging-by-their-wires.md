## What happened

On an early LED cooler retrofit, a few light bars were not seated properly in their mounts and ended up hanging by their wires. A tech had to go back. The customer also pointed out loose wire slack, exposed connectors and a leftover bracket.

## Why it happened

Parts were not fully seated and secured, wiring was not trimmed and dressed, and nobody stepped back to look before leaving.

## What we changed

- Seat and secure every part.
- Trim and dress wiring so splices are inside an enclosure.
- Remove leftover hardware and step back to look before you leave. A callback costs more than doing it right.

## What you would do

1. What does your final look-over include?
2. Who checks the crew's work on a project, and how often?
3. What does a callback cost the customer and us?
