## What happened

Our company had a near miss when a new vehicle went out towing a lift trailer with no brake controller installed.

## Why it happened

The new vehicle had no brake controller installed, and it went out towing a braked lift trailer anyway.

## What we changed

- No brake controller, no heavy braked trailer.
- Set the controller gain every time you hook up, per the company setup guide.
- Full trailer check every time: coupler latched and pinned, chains crossed, breakaway cable attached and charged, lights, tires and load.

## What you would do

1. How would you know a tow vehicle has a working brake controller?
2. What do you do if you are told to tow with a vehicle that is not set up?
3. Which trailer check is easiest to skip, and why?
