## What happened

Another vehicle made contact with one of our trucks. There was no real damage to our vehicle and the police would not come out, so the tech almost just drove away.

## Why it happened

With no visible damage and no police response, it felt like there was nothing to report.

## What we changed

- Report every contact to our insurance company, no matter how minor.
- If the police will not come out, call both insurance companies from the scene.
- Photograph everything, get names and plates, and fill out the company accident form. The other driver could later claim we hit them and that they were hurt.

## What you would do

1. Where are the blank accident forms in your vehicle?
2. What photos would you take at a minor bump?
3. Who do you call first, and who second?
