## What happened

On one of our jobs a new fixture was mounted with self-tapping screws straight into drywall. On another, a fixture had pulled loose and was held up only because the wire nuts happened to be tight.

## Why it happened

The fixtures were fastened to something that could not hold them: bare drywall with screws that do not grip it. Conductors and wire nuts ended up carrying the weight.

## What we changed

- Support the fixture from the box or the structure; conductors and wire nuts are never supports.
- Use toggles, or fasten to framing; carry a stud finder.
- Do the final tightening by hand until flush, then stop. Crumbling drywall will not hold even a toggle.

## What you would do

1. Who could have been under that fixture when it let go?
2. How do you tell the drywall will not hold?
3. What do you do if the approved method will not work on a housing?
