## What happened

Our technicians have found live wiring in fixtures they believed were dead.

## Why it happened

The causes our company has found: other circuits passing through the fixture or its wiring channel, incorrect original wiring, DC emergency battery packs, 24-hour night-light or emergency circuits, and a back-fed neutral shared with another circuit.

## What we changed

- Handle all wiring as if it were energized, even if you turned it off yourself.
- Our LOTO procedure ends with verification every time: test for absence of voltage live-dead-live at the work location.
- Isolate at the breaker or disconnect, and isolate or discharge stored energy such as battery packs.

## What you would do

1. Which of these causes have you run into?
2. Where in a fixture would you test to be sure it is dead?
3. How do you handle a fixture with an emergency battery pack?
