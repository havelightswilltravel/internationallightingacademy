## What happened

Someone screwed a small bracket to one of our lift-truck baskets, most likely to hang a drill. On another truck, holes had been drilled in the bottom of the bucket to let rain drain out.

## Why it happened

Both were well-meant "improvements" made to save time or deal with rain, without asking whether the manufacturer allowed them.

## What we changed

- Nothing is added to, drilled into or removed from a lift without the manufacturer's written approval.
- Either change can fail the annual inspection and take the truck out of service until repaired the manufacturer's way.
- Hang tools on the attachment points the maker provides; dump water by tipping the bucket as the manual allows.

## What you would do

1. Why might a few small screw holes matter in a basket?
2. What would you do if you found a modification on a truck you were assigned?
3. Where do you hang tools in the bucket you use?
