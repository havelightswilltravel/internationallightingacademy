## What happened

One of our medium-duty trucks stalled while being driven. Every dash light came on, and the driver had a
hard time bringing the truck to a full stop. No one was hurt. Our fleet service and the truck's
dealer both inspected it and found nothing wrong; the best guess was a tank of bad fuel.

## Why it happened

The cause was never confirmed. What made the stop hard was that, with the engine off, the brakes
lost their normal power assist. The truck has a backup electric brake-boost motor, but it only
helps if the driver keeps firm, steady pressure on the pedal.

## What we changed

- Every driver is taught: if the engine dies while moving, **press hard and hold the brake** so the
  backup boost motor kicks in, and steer for the shoulder. Steering will be heavy.
- Then follow the breakdown steps: flashers, triangles, wait clear of traffic, call your supervisor.
- A truck that stalls goes to the shop before it goes back out, even if it restarts.

## What you would do

1. Does the truck you drive most have a backup brake-boost motor? How would you know?
2. Your engine quits in the left lane of a busy road. Talk through the next 30 seconds.
3. The truck restarts fine. Why still report it?
