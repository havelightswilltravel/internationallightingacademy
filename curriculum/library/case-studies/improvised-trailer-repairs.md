## What happened

A tech found a trailer spare-tire mount broken and improvised a fix to hold the tire on. The photo made it to our safety meeting. In a separate case, a trailer jack was bent in a scrape.

## Why it happened

The tech was trying to keep working with the equipment he had. The bent jack looked minor, but the welds holding it on could have been damaged.

## What we changed

- The only safe repair is the manufacturer's original design. Remove the spare, or stop at a trailer shop.
- After any scrape, check the jack and tongue welds before the trailer goes back on the road.
- Report all trailer damage.

## What you would do

1. What would you have done with the broken mount?
2. What do you check after a trailer scrapes something?
3. Who do you call when trailer equipment breaks on the road?
