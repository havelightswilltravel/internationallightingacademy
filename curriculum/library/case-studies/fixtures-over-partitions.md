## What happened

On a project, some fixtures sat over cubicle walls with pipes above them or were screwed to the grid. The crew wanted to mark them and skip them.

## Why it happened

The fixtures were hard to reach, and the crew assumed hard meant skippable. The bid had already allowed extra time for them.

## What we changed

- Hard fixtures are usually in the bid; ask before assuming you can skip them.
- Fixtures that truly cannot be reached without damage are marked, left alone and listed on the recap with their location.
- Never force a fixture you might damage.

## What you would do

1. How would you find out whether hard fixtures are in the bid?
2. What goes on the recap for a skipped fixture?
3. What access equipment might have made these reachable?
