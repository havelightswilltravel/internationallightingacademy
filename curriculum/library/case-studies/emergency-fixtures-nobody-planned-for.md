## What happened

On several retrofits, emergency ballasts were in the fixtures but were not in the bid.

## Why it happened

The emergency equipment was not included in the bid, so the crew had no plan for it.

## What we changed

- Depending on the customer, the right answer was to label and leave them, rewire existing units, or replace them from a stock supply.
- Never start cutting out or adding emergency equipment without specific direction.
- Report how many and what type you left.

## What you would do

1. Why is cutting out an emergency ballast a life-safety problem?
2. Who gives the direction on emergency fixtures?
3. What should the survey have captured?
