## What happened

Our crews have come across industrial equipment with radiation warning labels on job sites.

## Why it happened

Lighting work takes us into industrial spaces where equipment we do not know may sit near our fixtures.

## What we changed

- Steer clear of equipment you do not recognize.
- Do not touch, move or work next to it until the site contact tells you it is safe.
- Ask about special hazards at check-in.

## What you would do

1. What would you do if a fixture you need is mounted above labeled equipment?
2. Who on site would you ask?
3. What other labels would make you stop and ask?
