## What happened

One customer's work-order portal has a visibility setting: notes and photos saved as internal-only never reach the customer, and that customer denies payment when completion photos are missing. Another customer questioned our paperwork because a manager's signature had no printed name beside it.

## Why it happened

Our records do not give more detail than this. The gaps were small: a portal visibility setting nobody checked, and a signature with no printed name. Both are fixed in a minute on site, or in weeks of back-and-forth afterward.

## What we changed

- Finish everything about the service on site, while still clocked in to that job.
- Upload photos to the company folder and the customer portal, set to customer-visible.
- Every signature gets the signer's printed name beside it.

## What you would do

1. What is the last thing you check before leaving a site?
2. How do you confirm a portal note is customer-visible?
3. What do you do when the customer's check-in system is down?
