## What happened

Company vehicles were driven off the pavement in spring and got stuck in the mud. After the
reminders started, it happened **twice more**, and each time the vehicle had to be pulled out by a
tow service. The reminder had to be repeated three times.

## Why it matters

Each tow-out costs the company money and a lost half-day, and an improvised recovery (spinning
the wheels, or a strap tied to whatever is handy) can damage the vehicle or hurt someone.

## How we deal with it

- **Company vehicles are not driven off road**, and spring mud is the worst of it.
- If a fixture can only be reached across grass or dirt, stop, take a photo and call your
  supervisor; we may need a different truck, ground mats or a different plan.
- If you are stuck: don't spin the wheels, don't accept a strap pull from a passer-by, call your
  supervisor for a proper tow, and note it on your pull sheet.

## What you would do

1. The pole you need is 30 ft onto a lawn. What are your options?
2. Why is a tow strap tied to a bumper dangerous?
3. Why do you think the reminder had to be given three times?
