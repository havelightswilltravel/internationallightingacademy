## What happened

A tech was dispatched to cover the photocell on a wall-mounted fixture so the lights would come on for testing. He used an 8-ft A-frame ladder, over-extended to reach, and fell. His injuries were minor. Because he reported it, it was brought to the safety meeting.

## Why it happened

The ladder was too short for the reach, so the tech had to over-extend to get to the photocell.

## What we changed

- The fall is now taught as the reason every van carries an extension ladder plus 12-ft, 8-ft and 6-ft ladders.
- The rule: right height ladder, or an extension ladder set at 4:1, or a lift.
- The event is used to show why reporting matters: a near miss that is never reported teaches no one.

## What you would do

1. Before you climb, how do you decide the ladder is tall enough for the reach?
2. What would you do if the right ladder is not on your truck?
3. What makes people hesitate to report a minor fall, and how do we fix that?
