## What happened

Two techs watched a manager pull the big multi-position ladder off the rack by himself to save a minute. He got it down without injury, and he put his own violation on the next safety meeting agenda so everyone would learn from it.

## Why it happened

Saving a minute felt more important than getting a second person.

## What we changed

- The team-lift rule applies to everyone, whatever their title.
- Anyone can stop an unsafe lift.
- Reporting your own mistake is what we want; discipline targets unsafe acts, not reports.

## What you would do

1. Would you have said something to the manager? How?
2. Which items on your truck need two people?
3. Why does it matter that the manager reported himself?
