## What happened

On one of our jobs, 13 of 20 wall packs quoted as standard turned out to be emergency (battery) units. The quote had to be redone, and the emergency replacements cost far more.

## Why it happened

Exterior emergency wall packs look like standard ones from the ground. Nobody checked each label or test button before the quote.

## What we changed

- Check each wall pack's label, test switch, indicator light or battery label.
- Count emergency units separately on every write-up and survey.
- Emergency units get emergency-rated replacements.

## What you would do

1. How do you spot an emergency wall pack from the ground?
2. What happens to the customer relationship when a quote has to be redone?
3. Where on the survey sheet do emergency units go?
