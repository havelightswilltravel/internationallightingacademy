## What happened

A customer's maintenance staff told us more than once that bugs "sure do love" the fixtures we maintain at their branches, and some locations believed we had never cleaned them. Our company has had repeated complaints about bugs, cobwebs and dust left in lenses after service calls.

## Why it happened

Fixtures were opened for service but not cleaned, or cleaning was done but not written in the notes.

## What we changed

- Cleaning fixtures is part of the service; every truck carries rags and cleaner on the pull sheet.
- Clean the fixture while it is open (locked out, lamps cooled).
- Write it in your notes so the customer and the office know.

## What you would do

1. How long does it take to wipe out a fixture that is already open?
2. What goes in your notes when you clean?
3. What else on a site have you noticed lately but not reported?
