## What happened

An office retrofit was bid for two-lamp fixtures, but many fixtures actually had four lamps.

## Why it happened

The survey did not match what was in the ceiling.

## What we changed

- The crew matched what was in each fixture, noted how many four-lamp fixtures they found and where, and reported the shortfall as soon as possible so material could be ordered.
- Rule: compare the real site to the survey on day one and report differences immediately, not when you run out.

## What you would do

1. How would you check the survey on day one?
2. Who do you tell, and what do you give them?
3. What could the surveyor have done differently?
