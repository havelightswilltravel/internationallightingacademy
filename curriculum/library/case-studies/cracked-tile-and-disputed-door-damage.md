## What happened

Our crews have left cracked floor tile behind a scissor lift. We have also risked being blamed for door damage we did not cause because nobody photographed the door before bringing a lift through.

## Why it happened

A slab scissor concentrates a lot of weight (our standard 19-ft slab scissor weighs about 3,300 lb) on tile, and existing door damage was not documented before work.

## What we changed

- Write up the lighter man lift, or send plywood to spread the scissor's load.
- Before bringing a lift through a single door, inspect and photograph the door and frame and tell the manager.
- Go slow and use a spotter every time you enter or exit with a lift.

## What you would do

1. How do you decide whether a floor needs protection?
2. What photos do you take before bringing a lift into a store?
3. Who do you tell about existing damage?
