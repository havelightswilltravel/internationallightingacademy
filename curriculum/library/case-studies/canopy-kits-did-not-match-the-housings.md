## What happened

On a gas station canopy LED retrofit, some existing housings did not match the sizes in the kit's mounting instructions, and some old housings were loose or rusted.

## Why it happened

Some of the existing housings were not the sizes the kit's mounting instructions were written for, and some were loose or rusted from age.

## What we changed

- The crew used the closest bracket fit and secured it, added an approved secondary restraint where the size did not match, did not install any kit they were unsure would stay secure, and called the supervisor.
- They drew a graph-paper diagram of each canopy showing north, the building and every problem fixture.
- Time spent securing housings was kept separate on the service order so the extra work could be billed.

## What you would do

1. Who could be under a loose canopy fixture?
2. What would you do if no approved restraint fits?
3. How does a canopy diagram help the office?
