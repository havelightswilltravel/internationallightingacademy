## What happened

On two jobs in two days for the same customer, our crew went past the not-to-exceed (NTE) amount. The overage could not be billed afterward. On another occasion an office manager asked a customer for an NTE increase by phone after the tech had left, and was turned down.

## Why it happened

The NTE was not tracked during the job, and the increase was not requested while the tech was on site, ready to finish.

## What we changed

- Know the NTE before you start and track labor, materials and equipment against it.
- Request an NTE increase from the job site; get the name of the person and ask for the approval by email.
- Before you write a return, ask whether you could finish today with one phone call.

## What you would do

1. How do you track time and materials against an NTE?
2. What would you say on the phone to request an increase?
3. Which returns could you have finished with one call?
