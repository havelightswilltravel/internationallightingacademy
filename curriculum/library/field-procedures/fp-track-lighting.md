## System components

| Component | What it does | Common failure |
|---|---|---|
| Track | Energized channel that carries power along its length | Dirty or bent conductors, loose joiners |
| Entrance fitting (live end / feed) | Connects building power to the track | Loose or burned connections - whole track out |
| Track fixtures (heads) | Clip into the track; hold lamps | Adapter contacts not engaging, internal switch off |
| Lamps | Halogen, metal halide or LED | End of life |
| Ballasts / drivers / transformers | Built into ballasted, LED or low-voltage heads | Failure; one head dark while others work |
| Wire | Feeds the entrance fitting; internal head wiring | Loose, heat damage |

## Safety first

- **LOTO and verify absence of voltage (live-dead-live)** before removing heads for repair, opening the entrance fitting or touching track conductors. Moving a head on an energized track is normal use, but never insert tools into the track slot while energized.
- Lamps and heads get hot; let them cool. Use a stable ladder.
- Energized voltage checks are **qualified persons only**. Check breakers **only if qualified; otherwise write it up.**

## Company troubleshooting procedure

1. **Verify there is at least one good lamp in the track** - this proves the track has power.
2. **If outages are sporadic, check for power at the sockets before replacing lamps or ballasts.**
3. **If there is no power at a socket but other fixtures on the track work, check for a switch on the track fixture, check for a good connection to the track, or move the fixture to a slightly different spot on the track.**
4. **If moving the fixture does not fix it, test continuity** (incandescent or halogen heads only, de-energized and removed from the track) **between the fixture's track contacts and the socket.** Expected: near 0 ohms; OL = broken connection.
5. **If the fixture is ballasted and other fixtures on the track work, replace the ballast** (or replace the head if the ballast is not serviceable).
6. **If the entire track is out, check power and connections at the entrance fitting** (locked out to open it).

## Escalate / write it up when

- No power at the entrance fitting.
- Burned track or entrance fitting, or a track that is overloaded (total wattage over the track rating).
- Line-voltage wiring problems above the ceiling.

## Parts & information

- Identify the track system type - **H, J and L** style tracks are not interchangeable. Note brand and number of circuits.
- Head: lamp type and wattage, ballast/driver model, color, adapter type. Photograph the head and adapter.
- Bring a sample head or adapter if the brand is unknown.

## Document on the work order

Track location, heads affected, readings, parts replaced, track type, and anything written up.
