*Guide for supervisors and crew leads - based on how our company has run its monthly safety
meetings.*

## Why it matters

Our company holds a safety meeting every month, before the crew heads out. The same
hazards come back every year - ice in winter, heat in summer, ladders all year - so the
meeting follows a **seasonal calendar**, and hands-on inspections are built into it. The
talks in this library are written to be read aloud in about 10 minutes each.

## The meeting format we use

1. **Safety manual review** - read one section of the company safety manual (rules,
   enforcement, PPE, ladders, electrical, LOTO, fire prevention, HazCom, driving).
2. **Safety training** - one or two toolbox talks from this library, often with a short
   training video.
3. **Hands-on inspection** when scheduled - PPE and hand tools, or ladders, ladder racks and
   extension cords (two-person inspection, forms turned in before the crew leaves).
4. **Lessons learned** - photos from our own jobs: near misses, damage, good catches. Ask
   "What would you do differently?" and let the crew answer. No names on the screen.
5. **Other safety concerns** - open floor. Anyone can raise anything.
6. **Agenda items** - task training and field reminders (paperwork, parts, customer issues).
7. **Calendar** - closures, inventory day, time-off reminders.

Keep a sign-in sheet; attendance is training documentation.

## Suggested annual calendar

| Month | Safety training | Hands-on |
|---|---|---|
| January | Fall protection; working above the public | PPE and hand-tool inspection |
| February | Distracted and defensive driving | - |
| March | Electrical shock and arc flash; material handling | - |
| April | Ladder selection and setup | Ladder and cord inspection |
| May | Heat stress; tool and equipment rules | PPE and hand-tool inspection |
| June | Vehicle pre-trip (DVIR) and defensive driving; HazCom | - |
| July | Lockout/tagout review; material handling; incident reporting | - |
| August | Ladders and extension cords; vehicle accident procedure; DVIR | Ladder and cord inspection |
| September | Fall protection review; bucket trucks and jibs; tools | PPE and hand-tool inspection |
| October | Winter driving; extension cords through doorways | - |
| November | Material handling; LOTO; cold weather | - |
| December | Ladder safety; cold weather and getting stranded | Ladder and cord inspection |

Fit task-training talks (meter first, drywall mounting, flashing fixtures, write-ups, NTEs,
paperwork, recycling, truck stock) into the agenda-items slot as problems show up in the field.

## Tips for the person leading

- Use photos from your own jobs, with customer names and people's faces removed.
- Ask questions instead of lecturing - "Opinions?" works.
- Admit your own mistakes. Crews respect a leader who puts their own violation on the agenda.
- Record follow-up actions and close them out at the next meeting.
