## Why it matters

A five-minute walk-around catches the cracked windshield, the dead marker light or the
expired extinguisher **before** it becomes a ticket, a breakdown or a crash. The driver vehicle
inspection report (DVIR) is **company policy every day**, whether or not a given trip falls
under DOT rules.

## Key points

- **Every vehicle and trailer, every shift.** Complete the DVIR in the company app; log in and
  out each shift and set your status to off duty at the end of the day. Nobody drives a
  company vehicle until they are trained on the DVIR and logs. Report defects to your
  supervisor **before** use.
- **On your person / in the truck:** driver's license, medical card if required, registration
  and insurance, inspection paperwork.
- **The walk-around:** horn; windshield (no cracks in the driver's view), wipers and fluid;
  mirrors; **no warning lights on the dash**; service and parking brakes; steering; lights and
  reflectors; three reflective triangles; fire extinguisher mounted, charged and current;
  tires, wheels and rims; loads on flatbeds secured or covered; registered weight greater than
  actual weight (including any trailer).
- **DOT jurisdiction.** Federal rules generally apply to vehicles over 10,001 lb crossing state
  lines; in-state thresholds vary by state. Our company keeps following the federal standards
  everywhere because the rules can change and a trip can cross a state line.
- **Inspect days before an out-of-town trip** so there is time to fix defects.
- **Parking:** set the parking brake every time; trucks over 15,000 lb GVW are also chocked on
  an incline. Equipment left near a road overnight needs lights or reflectors.

## Warning signs you must act on

- **Battery light at start-up** may mean a broken serpentine belt - no belt, no coolant
  circulation. Shut the engine off and call it in.
- **Check-engine light or any vehicle problem:** tell your supervisor right away and note it
  on your pull sheet.
- **Engine dies while moving:** press hard and hold the brake - some trucks have a backup
  electric brake-boost motor that kicks in.
- **Diesel exhaust filter "regen" request:** accept it and pull over for the 5-10 minutes it
  takes. Declining just passes the problem to the next driver until the truck goes into limp
  mode.
- **Check the oil regularly** (a good habit: every fourth fuel stop). One of our vans was found
  three quarts low.
- **Rock chips:** when you hear a hit, inspect at the next stop, circle the chip with a marker
  and note it on your pull sheet - chips can be repaired before they spread.

## Discussion questions

1. What do you do if the DVIR finds a burned-out marker light before a long trip?
2. Why does the company follow DOT rules even when a trip is not under DOT jurisdiction?
3. What does a broken serpentine belt look like from the driver's seat?
