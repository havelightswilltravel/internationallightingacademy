*Toolbox talk (about 5 minutes) - an operations issue from our company's monthly safety meetings,
covered as how we handle it when it comes up.*

## The issue

Metal halide lamps and ballasts are being discontinued, and spare lenses for older fixtures are
running out. A tech on an HID or broken-lens call often cannot fix it with what is on the truck.

## Why it matters

Each call that ends without the information the office needs becomes a second trip. Some
customers also have their own rules (for example, no ballast-bypass lamps in exterior fixtures),
and a repair that breaks the customer's standard has to be redone.

## How we deal with it

- **Failed HID with no truck-stock fix?** Use an approved LED replacement lamp only where it fits
  the fixture and the customer allows it, wired per LT2-C05 and labeled. If in doubt, write it up.
- **What to document:** measured supply voltage, fixture location on the property, pole type,
  whether a photocell is needed, photos, and the count of matching fixtures.
- **Lens needed?** Take a precise measurement and a count. For a wrap lens, trace it or bring a
  sample and photograph the fixture: if no lens can be found, the office may need to quote new
  fixtures for the whole area.
- **Who to call:** your supervisor, when you are unsure of the customer's standard.
- **Don't** install a bypass lamp a customer has not approved, or force a lamp that does not fit.

## Discussion questions

1. What do you check before installing an LED replacement lamp in an HID fixture?
2. Why count all the fixtures in the area when you only need one lens?
