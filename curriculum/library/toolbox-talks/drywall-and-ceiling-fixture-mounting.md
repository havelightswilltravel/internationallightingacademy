*Toolbox talk (about 10 minutes) - adapted from our company's monthly safety meetings.*

## Why it matters

A surface fixture that pulls out of a ceiling can fall on someone. Our meetings have shown
photos of fixtures fastened to drywall with self-tapping screws, fixtures crooked or off
center, exposed wiring, and one fixture that was **hanging only by its wire nuts.** These are
safety problems and workmanship problems, and the customer sees every one.

## Key points - the company drywall procedure

- **No self-tapping metal screws or wood screws into drywall alone.** They have no holding
  power in gypsum and will pull out.
- **Toggle bolts in drywall: snug, not tight.** If you use a drill or impact driver, use it only
  to bring the toggle close; **do the final tightening by hand** until the fixture is flush with
  the ceiling - then stop. Tighten more only if the fixture is still loose. Overtightening
  crushes the drywall and the toggle loses its grip.
- **Crumbling or water-damaged drywall?** Not even a toggle will hold. Fasten to the framing
  (joists or blocking) instead - carry and use a stud finder - or support the fixture from the
  structure as the manufacturer requires.
- **Support the fixture from the box or structure, never from the conductors.** Wire nuts and
  wires are not supports. Heavy fixtures need a box or support rated for the weight.
- **Wiring must be concealed** - inside the box, fixture or a raceway. Exposed splices and loose
  cable under a fixture are a safety issue.
- **Center the fixture** on the box, the room feature or the old footprint. If you cannot center
  it, get approval before you install it off center.
- **Keep rows straight.** Sales may say "one for one," but if the existing layout is crooked and
  you can easily put the new fixtures in a straight line, ask. Question it before you install,
  not after.

## What happened

On one job a new fixture was mounted with self-tapping screws straight into drywall. On
another, the fixture had pulled loose and was held up only because the wire nuts were tight.
Either one could have dropped a fixture onto a customer. Toggles or framing - and hand-tighten
the last turn.
