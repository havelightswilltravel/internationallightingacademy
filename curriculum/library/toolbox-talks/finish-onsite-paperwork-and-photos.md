*Toolbox talk (about 10 minutes) - adapted from our company's monthly safety meetings.*

## Why it matters

Customers deny payment when photos are missing, signatures are unreadable or check-in rules
are broken. And every note the office has to chase down costs time we are not paid for. Our
company rule is simple: **everything about the service gets done while you are still on site
and still clocked in to that job.**

## Key points - before you put the truck in drive

- Work order complete (everything except time and pre-pulled parts), with **exact part codes**
  for every part used - "24 V driver" is not a part code.
- **Photos uploaded** to the company photo folder for that service order **and** to the
  customer's work-order portal when they use one. In portals, make sure notes and photos are
  set to be **visible to the customer**, not internal only.
- **Before-and-after photos** when the customer asks for them: take them from the same spot,
  at the same height, so they compare.
- Return sheet written, parts and equipment listed.
- **Signatures on all paperwork** - with the signer's **printed name** next to the signature.
  Some customers question every unreadable signature.
- **Check-in/check-out (phone IVR or app) done** the way the customer requires. If the system
  fails, call the dispatcher or the customer's service desk and **write it in your notes.**
- Receipts submitted, equipment stored, van organized.
- **Equipment and supplies recorded.** Write down equipment you used beyond your standard
  ladders and tools (lifts, tracers, large ladders, generator, torches), and job supplies such
  as rags, caulk, spray paint, parking fees and batteries. If it costs us, the customer should
  see it.

## Work order vs. return sheet

- **Work order = what you did.** End it with "see return" if more work is needed.
- **Return sheet = what is still needed:** parts with codes and quantities, equipment, number
  of people, time estimate, access notes and photos.

Keeping them separate makes quotes faster and keeps the customer's record clean.

## What happened

One customer's work-order portal has a visibility setting: notes and photos saved as
internal-only never reach the customer, and that customer denies payment when completion
photos are missing. Another customer questioned our paperwork because a manager's signature
had no printed name beside it. Both are fixed in a minute on site - or in weeks of
back-and-forth afterward.

## Discussion questions

1. What is the last thing you check before leaving a site?
2. What makes a return sheet easy for the office to turn into a quote?
3. What do you do when a customer's check-in system is down?
