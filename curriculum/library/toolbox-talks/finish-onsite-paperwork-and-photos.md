*Toolbox talk (about 5 minutes) - an operations issue from our company's monthly safety meetings,
covered as how we handle it when it comes up.*

## The issue

The work is done, but photos are not uploaded, a signature has no printed name, part codes are
vague ("24 V driver"), or the customer's check-in system did not work.

## Why it matters

Some customers **deny payment** when photos are missing or paperwork is unclear, and every note
the office has to chase later is time nobody is paid for. Fixing it on site takes minutes; fixing
it afterward takes weeks. See case study *Photos the Customer Never Saw*.

## How we deal with it

- **Finish the paperwork on site, while you are still clocked in to that job:** exact part codes,
  photos uploaded where the customer can see them, signatures with printed names, the return
  sheet if more work is needed.
- **Work order = what you did. Return sheet = what is still needed.**
- **Customer check-in (phone or app) fails?** Call the dispatcher or the customer's service desk
  right then, and **write it in your notes** with the time and who you spoke to.
- **Record equipment and job supplies you used** beyond your standard ladders and tools, so the
  office can bill them. Ask your supervisor if you are unsure what counts.
- **Don't** leave the site planning to "send it tonight," and don't text photos to a manager in
  place of uploading them.

## Discussion questions

1. What is the last thing you check before leaving a site?
2. What do you do when a customer's check-in system is down?
