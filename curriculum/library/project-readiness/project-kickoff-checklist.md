*Company checklist, generalized from the company's past project checklists. The crew leader fills it in
and hands it to the supervisor with the completed paperwork. Use it with the **Project Specifics Sheet
Template**. Taught in LT2-C12 Lessons 3-5.*

Project / service order: ____________ Crew leader: ____________ Start date: ____________

## A. Before leaving the shop
- [ ] Read the project specifics sheet, survey and service order. Questions listed and asked.
- [ ] Survey count compared with the material list. Differences reported to: ________
- [ ] Site contact and after-hours supervisor contact known.
- [ ] Start/stop times, access, keys/badges/alarm and lock-up procedure known.
- [ ] Lifts/ladders for each area confirmed; fit, elevator and storage checked.
- [ ] How material gets to the site agreed with supervisor.
- [ ] Tools loaded: drill, bits, meter and lockout kit **for every person**; chargers; task lights.
- [ ] Supplies loaded: spare sockets, connectors, disconnects, wire, screws, approved securing hardware,
      plastic sheeting, cleaner, rags, clean gloves, recycle boxes, tape, carts, trash cans, labels.
- [ ] Shop equipment checked out with: ________ Date: ________
- [ ] New fixtures/kits inspected; damaged cartons opened, photographed and reported.
- [ ] Load and trailer secured; hitch, chains and lights checked.

## B. Kickoff day
- [ ] Checked in with site manager: ________ (name). Logistics, times and off-limits areas reviewed.
- [ ] Storage location for material and equipment: ________
- [ ] Location for spent material/recycling: ________
- [ ] Walked the job with the manager; **before photos** taken and sent.
- [ ] Game plan agreed (start area, high-traffic areas, what must look normal by opening).
- [ ] Material counted against the order; missing/damaged material photographed and reported **immediately**.
- [ ] Sample install done by crew leader and approved. Date: ________
- [ ] Staging, carts, recycle boxes and barricades set up before the first fixture comes down.
- [ ] Project vehicle returned if directed; crew reports directly to the site.

## C. Every shift
- [ ] Checked in and out.
- [ ] Morning call-in / update to supervisor.
- [ ] JHA reviewed; lifts inspected; areas below lifts and ladders barricaded.
- [ ] Every circuit locked out and verified live-dead-live before work on conductors.
- [ ] Desks, equipment and open food covered.
- [ ] Production checked against the bid expectation; if behind, reasons written down.
- [ ] Crew leader QC check of everyone's work; problems fixed right away.
- [ ] Step back and look from the floor: alignment, fit, color, no wires showing, no fingerprints.
- [ ] Counts recorded: fixtures by area, kits/drivers, disconnects, sockets, extra hardware, truck stock used.
- [ ] Skipped fixtures marked and located on the recap, with reason.
- [ ] Damage found or caused: manager told (name), photos taken, supervisor notified.
- [ ] Out-of-scope requests written down and passed to supervisor (not just done).
- [ ] Lamp boxes closed and taped both ends, guards removed first; PCB ballasts separate and counted.
- [ ] Site cleaned; equipment stored out of the way; circuits restored and lights tested.
- [ ] Site contact told what was done and what is next.
- [ ] Daily recap sent (done, remaining, counts, problems, how the shift went).

## E. Closeout
- [ ] Final counts on the service order; survey corrected; skipped fixtures listed.
- [ ] Leftover good material counted, packed, labeled and kept apart from recycle material. Manager
      shown location: ________ (name).
- [ ] Recycle pallets stacked, shrink-wrapped, labeled (1 of __) and **photographed**. Count: ____ Location: ________
- [ ] Recycle and return pickups scheduled. Date: ________
- [ ] Rental lifts called off. Call-off number: ________
- [ ] Final walk with manager: ________ (name). Punch items fixed.
- [ ] **After photos** taken and sent.
- [ ] Service order and customer completion forms signed (and stamped if required).
- [ ] Paperwork photos: page alone, flat, in focus, readable.
- [ ] Site and staging area clean; our materials removed unless told otherwise.
- [ ] Vehicle and trailer cleaned out; equipment returned to the shop.
- [ ] Time cards and receipts submitted.
- [ ] Feedback for the next sheet given to supervisor (what worked, what didn't, what was missing).
- [ ] Completed paperwork and this checklist hand delivered to supervisor.

> **Safety:** If you are not certain a fixture or housing is secure, do not leave it. If a site rule is
> stricter than ours, follow it or stop and call your supervisor. Fatigue is a hazard: follow work-hour
> and driving limits and speak up if you are too tired to work safely.
