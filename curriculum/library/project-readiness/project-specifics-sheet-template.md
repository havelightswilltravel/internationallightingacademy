## 14. Be the professional
Our company is the lighting professional on the job. Write up and explain to the site contact anything
that would make the finished job look unprofessional, even if outside the scope: yellowed or broken
lenses, missing guards, damaged fixtures, objects blocking light, fingerprints.

## 15. Vehicle and closeout
- Keep the project vehicle clean as you go. At the end, clean out vehicle and trailer, return all equipment.
- Closeout: walk the job with the manager, get the service order signed (and stamped if required),
  and hand in paperwork with the Project Kickoff Checklist.

> **Safety:** Lock out and verify absence of voltage live-dead-live on every circuit before working on
> conductors. Inspect lifts every shift and barricade below. Follow any site safety rule that is stricter
> than ours, or stop and call your supervisor. Follow work-hour and driving limits.
